# CUAD attribution

Selected category definitions, source annotations and derived experimental records originate from **CUAD v1**, The Atticus Project.

- Authors: Dan Hendrycks, Collin Burns, Anya Chen, Spencer Ball (NeurIPS 2021).
- Source: https://github.com/The-Atticus-Project/cuad
- Fixed source revision: `67faa0e6023b04fcaae6cc09497ab00e5d63a2a2`.
- Dataset license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
- Changes: nine-category selection, bounded excerpt windows, source-family grouping, exact-context deduplication and deterministic sampling; original expert evidence wording is preserved. Negative excerpts and model predictions are derived experimental material.

Attribution applies to `configs/cuad-category-definitions.json`, the generated CUAD task datasets and public experimental records under `docs/validation/cuad-v2-attempt/`. Category definitions retain upstream wording. Model-generated outputs are predictions and are not expert labels.
